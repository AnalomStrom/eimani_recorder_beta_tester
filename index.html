Testing Requirements

Here is what you need to know before joining the beta test.

20+

At Least 20 Testers

We need at least 20 active testers trying the app on different Android phones.

15d

15 Consecutive Days

Stay opted in to the Closed Test for 15 days in a row without leaving early.

25–45m

25–45 Minutes Per Day

Use the app for 25 to 45 minutes each day. You can split this time into smaller sessions throughout the day.

Please note: The 20 testers / 15-day target is our beta program requirement. Google Play may have separate production-access requirements. Please follow Analom Studio's instructions throughout the program.

How to Join

Follow these 5 simple steps after you register.

  1. 1

    Use Your Registered Gmail

    Sign in on your phone using the exact same Gmail account you gave us during registration.

  2. 2

    Open the Closed Test Link

    After we approve your Gmail address, open the Google Play opt-in link we share with you.

  3. 3

    Join the Closed Test

    Tap the button on the Google Play page to accept the invite and join the Closed Test.

  4. 4

    Install Eimani Recorder

    After joining, use the Google Play link to install Eimani Recorder on your Android phone.

  5. 5

    Open the App and Start Testing

    Launch Eimani Recorder, try the recording tools every day, and note how everything works.

Important: Use the same Google account for joining, downloading, installing, and testing the app. Stay opted in for the entire testing period.

Features to Test

Tap each card to see what to try and what problems to look out for.

1. Screen Recording

Test: Start recording, stop recording, save the video, and play it back.

Check: Recording failures, missing or broken videos, sudden stops, and video playback issues.

2. Audio Recording (internal, mic, internal + mic)

Test: Record with internal audio, microphone only, and internal audio + mic together.

Check: Sound clarity, audio and video staying in sync, missing sound, low volume, or recording errors.

3. Floating Controls

Test: Use the floating start/stop and pause/resume buttons while recording.

Check: How fast buttons respond, where they sit on screen, how easy they are to see, and if they get in your way.

4. Video Quality

Test: Record videos using the different resolution and quality settings on your device.

Check: Blurry video, dropped frames, stuttering, wrong screen size, or weird visual glitches.

5. Performance (games, normal use, video playback)

Test: Record while playing mobile games, using everyday apps, and watching videos.

Check: FPS drops, game lag, phone getting hot, unexpected pauses, or your phone slowing down.

6. Stability

Test: Use the app normally across all your daily testing sessions.

Check: App crashes, background service stopping, lost recordings, permission pop-up bugs, or strange behavior.

Report every reproducible problem.

Testing Rules

Please follow these simple rules during the 15-day beta program.

  • Give honest feedback based on what you actually tested on your phone.
  • Report even minor bugs, especially problems that happen more than once.
  • Stay opted in to the Closed Test for all 15 consecutive days.
  • Do not uninstall Eimani Recorder during the test unless necessary.
  • Use the exact same Gmail account from registration to finish.
  • Send your feedback privately through one of the channels listed above.
  • Do not copy feedback or bug reports from other testers.
